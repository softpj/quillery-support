Quillery Support

Get help setting up your journal, importing schedules or using Quillery on your devices.

Quick answers

How do I get started?

Open Settings → Help in Quillery to take the quick guide.

How do I import a schedule?

Open Journal → Day’s schedule and choose Photo or screenshot or Browse files. Check the dates and times before adding events.

Why isn’t a device feature working?

Check Quillery’s permissions in your device’s Settings. Features such as Calendar, Reminders and Apple Health need the permissions you choose to allow.

How do I back up my journal?

Open Settings → Sync, sharing and backups in Quillery and export a backup before resetting your journal or changing devices.
