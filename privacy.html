Quillery Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. Quillery was previously called Folio; this policy covers both names. Quillery keeps your journal on your device. Nobody else can read it, including us.

What Quillery stores, and where

Your journal (entries, habits, trackers, collections and settings) is saved on your iPhone or iPad, protected by the device’s own encryption. If you turn on iCloud sync, Quillery keeps a copy in your private iCloud database; only your Apple Account can read it, and we cannot. Quillery also keeps copies of your journal on your device for as long as you keep the app, so you can go back to an earlier day: one from the start of each day for the last month, then one a week for a year, then one a month. Your journal itself is never removed by Quillery.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Quillery reads the categories you choose, such as steps, sleep, water or cycle data, to fill in habits and trackers, and writes the cycle records you ask it to. Health data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app.

Widgets, Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Watch

Widgets show only what you choose to share in Quillery’s Privacy settings, and that information stays on your device in a space shared with the widgets. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled on your device. The Apple Watch app receives only the habits you choose to send it.

Calendar, microphone, camera, photos and Face ID

Quillery asks before reading your calendar, listening for dictation, using the camera or opening a photo. Dictation uses Apple’s speech recognition, which may process audio on Apple’s servers under Apple’s privacy policy. Face ID and Touch ID are handled by iOS; Quillery never receives your face, fingerprint or passcode.

Purchases

Quillery is free for the first month, then a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and keeps your card details; Quillery never sees them. Quillery checks your purchase with the App Store on your device and stores nothing about it anywhere else. If a subscription ends, your journal stays on your device and you can still export it.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Quillery does not send your journal to us or to anyone else.

Export or reset your journal

Open Settings → Journal data in Quillery to export or reset your journal. If iCloud sync is on, Quillery will try to sync the reset. Copies you exported are yours to keep or remove.

Contacting support

If you send a request using the support form, we store your email address and message details in a private support database only as long as needed to respond. Please leave out private journal entries, health details and passwords.

Contact Quillery support if you have a privacy question or want to ask about a previous message.

Changes

We will update this page if anything above changes.
